## Vidkit direction
Use an original consistent identity with high-contrast text, restrained motion and one focal explanation at a time. Reuse layouts, not another channel's branding or scripts.

Six initial scene families:
- Headline or key statistic.
- Tool identity and relevant capabilities.
- Source-page crop with focused emphasis.
- Process diagram.
- Comparison with matched conditions.
- Limitation and takeaway.

Give each scene a job: establish, demonstrate, explain, compare or qualify. Avoid decorative motion that competes with reading. Keep evidence legible; crop relevant source sections rather than showing unreadably dense pages.

Use real historical data for growth charts. With only a current total, show a total or clearly conceptual visual. Save source and asset provenance in the handoff.

Target portrait 1080×1920 at 30 fps. Check compositions with a Shorts interface overlay and at phone size; keep critical information clear of right-side controls and lower metadata. Treat safe-area guides as configurable, since platform overlays vary.
